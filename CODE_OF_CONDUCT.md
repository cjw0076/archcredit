# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, provide constructive feedback, and focus disagreements on evidence and ideas.

Acceptable behavior includes listening to criticism, acknowledging mistakes, respecting boundaries, and considering the effects of your words. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks, and disclosure of private information without permission are unacceptable.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn contributors, or restrict participation. They will consider the context, impact, and whether behavior repeats. Maintainers are subject to the same standards.

For an incident, contact a repository maintainer through an available private GitHub channel. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting private contact without identifying affected people or describing sensitive details. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for security vulnerabilities when enabled. Do not post private incident details in public issues. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy and explain enforcement decisions to affected parties where appropriate.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
